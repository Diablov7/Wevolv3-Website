A big post is not a new user

A creator can put your token in front of half a million people and leave your numbers exactly where they were. The audience scrolls, impressions climb, and a week later nothing has moved. The problem was never awareness. It was that all that attention had nowhere to go and no reason to act. We wrote more about why this keeps happening in how crypto KOL marketing has evolved.

What actually changes

The question stops being how many people saw the post. It becomes how many of them are still around a month later.

We point the right voices at the people most likely to act, and we stay close enough to what happens next to move budget the moment something isn't working. You end up with reach you can trace to real users, in the markets and languages they are actually in, instead of a folder of screenshots and a number that never turned into anything.

Questions we hear a lot

A big influencer wants a lot of money. Is it worth it?

Usually not. A huge account in the wrong community costs more and leaves you with less than a smaller voice whose audience actually trusts them. The size of the number matters much less than who is behind it. If you want the market numbers behind that answer, we published what Web3 KOLs actually charge, from 861 creators.

How do I know it worked?

Because you can see it. We tie the attention back to real actions, so you are not left staring at view counts trying to guess whether any of it reached a person who mattered.

How do you deal with fake followers and bots?

We look at who is really behind an audience before anyone goes near your campaign, and we keep watching once it is live. Voices that don't land don't stay in.

Can you reach users outside the usual English-speaking crowd?

Yes. Your users are spread across languages and regions, and the voices we reach for are too, with real strength in the markets most providers ignore.

Is this a one-off or something ongoing?

Whatever fits. Some projects need a sharp push around a moment. Others need a steady presence that keeps the right people paying attention. We shape it around where you are.
