Crypto KOL marketing FAQ

How do you pick the right KOLs?

By fit, not fame. We match creators to your chain, region and audience, vet them for real (not bot) engagement, and prioritize the ones whose audience actually acts. A mid-size KOL with a real community beats a big account with hollow reach.

How do you measure KOL results?

With attribution, not vibes. Tracked links and codes tie each creator to real outcomes: clicks, signups, quest completions and trades, so you know which voices moved the needle and which just spent budget.

How do you avoid fake engagement and bot followers?

We vet every creator's audience quality and engagement authenticity before they go in a campaign, and we watch performance live so underperformers get cut fast.

Can you run campaigns across regions and languages?

Yes. We run KOLs across major crypto regions and languages, including strong presence in emerging markets, so your message lands natively where your users are.

Is it one campaign or ongoing?

Either. Tailored by design: a focused launch push or an always-on KOL engine, scaled up or down as you need.
