Development FAQ

Do you do development and marketing, or just one?

Both, and that is the point. Most projects juggle a marketing agency and a separate dev shop that never talk to each other. We run growth and build the product with one team, so the campaign and the thing it points to actually match.

What can you build?

Marketing sites and dApp frontends, full-stack web apps, iOS and Android apps, Telegram bots for onboarding and gating, AI agents and automation, plus smart contracts, backend and infrastructure. From an MVP to a production launch.

What is your stack?

Modern and boring where it should be: Next.js, React and Tailwind on the web, native or cross-platform on mobile, and the right chain tooling for your protocol. We optimize for something you can maintain after we hand it over, not a demo that breaks in a month.

How does development work with the growth side?

They run together. The team building your product sees the same data and goals as the team driving users to it. No handoffs, no finger-pointing, no waiting two weeks for a landing page before a launch.

How is it priced?

Per project, tailored to scope. Tell us what you are building and we come back with a plan and a number. You can run it as a standalone build or as part of a full growth partnership.
