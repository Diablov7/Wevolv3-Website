Community building FAQ

What does community building actually cover?

Three things that run every day: moderation and spam control on Telegram and Discord, an ambassador program that turns your best holders into contributors, and social media management that keeps a consistent voice across platforms. It is the always-on layer under everything else.

How do you handle spam and scammers?

Real-time monitoring plus AI anti-spam bots covering all 24 hours, backed by Sentry Bot for anti-spam and anti-scam analysis. Fake admins, impersonators and drainer links get caught before your holders do, so the group stays clean without a moderator staring at it all night.

What is the ambassador program?

End to end: we curate and onboard ambassadors, train them, track points and rewards on an automated dashboard, and send you a monthly report covering growth, contributions and ROI per person. You get a real contributor layer instead of a giveaway that attracts bots.

Why does an active community matter so much?

An empty or spammy Telegram is the first red flag an investor sees. A quiet group reads as a dead project before anyone looks at the chart. Keeping the room active, clean and moderated protects conversions at the exact moment people are deciding whether to trust you.

Can I run this without the rest of the growth engine?

Yes. Community operations work as a standalone layer, or as the foundation the rest of the engine (KOL, on-chain visibility, PR) builds on. Most projects start here because nothing else lands if the room they send people to is a mess.
