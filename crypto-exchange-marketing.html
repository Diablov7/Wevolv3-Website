Exchange marketing FAQ

How is marketing a crypto exchange different from marketing a token?

A token launch is one moment; an exchange is an ongoing product that has to earn trades every single day. Exchange marketing is about sustained user acquisition, trust, liquidity signals and retention — not just launch-day noise. We build always-on community, KOL and PR engines that keep new traders arriving and existing traders active.

Can you drive real trading volume, not just impressions?

Yes. We tie campaigns to on-platform actions — signups, KYC completions, first trades — using KOL attribution and tracked links, so you can see which channels produce traders rather than vanity reach. Volume follows real, retained users, and that's what we optimize for.

Do you work with both centralized (CEX) and decentralized (DEX) exchanges?

Both. For CEXs we focus on trust, listings buzz and compliant acquisition. For DEXs we lean into community, incentives and on-chain-native channels. The strategy differs, but the goal is the same: durable users and volume.

What does the modular model mean for an exchange?

You pay for the services you actually need — spin up KOL campaigns for a listing push, add PR before a raise, or run always-on community ops — and scale each lever up or down as the market moves, without a bloated full-service retainer.

How fast can you start?

Most engagements kick off within a week. We start with a short strategy sprint to map your funnel and priorities, then activate the highest-leverage channels first.
