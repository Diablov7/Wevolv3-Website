Design & motion FAQ

What do you design?

Visual assets your community actually uses: custom static sticker packs, animated stickers, 3D spinning emojis and branded GIFs. All brand-aligned and built for Telegram first, then wherever else your community lives.

Why stickers and emojis instead of a normal ad?

Because they get used. A holder who reacts with your sticker or drops your GIF in another chat is marketing you for free, over and over. Static ads get scrolled past. A sticker that lives in someone's tray is brand recall that keeps working.

How fast is delivery?

A static sticker pack of 20-plus stickers ships in about six business days with up to three revisions. Smaller items like 3D emojis and GIFs turn around faster. We keep it brand-aligned so it drops straight into your existing identity.

Will it match our existing brand?

Yes. Everything is built to extend your identity, not compete with it. If you have a brand system we work inside it. If you do not, we can shape one alongside the design and motion work so it stays coherent.

Can this run with the rest of the engine?

Design and motion pairs naturally with community and engagement work. The assets give your moderators, ambassadors and campaigns something to react with, which makes every other layer feel more alive.
