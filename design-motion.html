Questions we hear a lot

Our visuals look a bit amateur. Does that really cost us?

It costs you the people who never gave you a chance. In crypto, cheap-looking design reads as a warning sign, and most of them decide before they read a word. Looking legit is the price of being taken seriously.

Why does design matter more than one more ad?

An ad gets seen once. How you look follows you into every chat, every reaction, every screenshot someone shares. If that impression says trustworthy, it works for you around the clock. If it says risky, no amount of spend fixes it.

We already have a logo. Isn't that enough?

A logo is where trust starts, not where it lands. People judge you on everything around it: the reactions, the animations, the way it all holds together. We make sure the whole picture reads like a project that is here to stay.

Will this actually match who we are?

Yes. We build on your identity instead of fighting it. If you have a brand already, we work inside it. If you do not, we shape one so nothing about you looks improvised or borrowed.

How does this fit with the rest of our marketing?

It makes everything else land harder. When your project looks credible, the campaigns, the community and the KOLs all get met with trust instead of suspicion. It is the layer that decides whether the rest gets a fair hearing.
