Questions we hear a lot

We're close to launch. Is it too late to matter?

It depends how close. The audience that shows up on launch day is built in the weeks before it, so the earlier you start the warmer the room. If the date is near, we tell you honestly what can still move the needle instead of taking your money and hoping.

What happens if we launch and nobody shows up?

That is the outcome this whole thing exists to prevent. A quiet open tells the market there's nothing here, and that impression is very hard to undo. We spend the weeks before making sure the right people already know you and are waiting, so the room is full when it counts.

How do you make sure the attention is real and not bots?

We care about who is actually watching, not the size of a number. KOLs are chosen for fit with your chain and community, and measured on real signups and participation, so what you see on launch day is people who might stay, not farmed noise.

Everyone talks about launch day. What about after?

A launch buys attention. Keeping it is the harder part, and where most projects quietly die. We plan for the weeks after from the start, so the people who show up on day one have a reason to still be around a month later.

How does working with you actually work?

We turn on only what your launch needs and scale it around the moment that matters. Senior people on your launch, not a bloated retainer, so the effort goes where it changes the outcome.
