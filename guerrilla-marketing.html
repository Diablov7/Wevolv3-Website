Guerrilla marketing FAQ

What is guerrilla marketing in crypto?

Coordinated, high-volume tactics that make your project impossible to miss across the surfaces crypto lives on: Twitter, Telegram, Discord, TikTok, CoinMarketCap comments and /biz/. The goal is to saturate the timeline and own your cashtag so the conversation feels alive wherever people look.

How is this different from a KOL campaign?

KOL is a few big voices. Guerrilla is many coordinated touchpoints: reply networks, investor-chat outreach, native threads and community ops working in volume. They complement each other, one builds authority through names, the other builds ambient presence through saturation.

Which surfaces do you cover?

Twitter through an AI reply engine and a verified reply network, Telegram and Discord through community ops and investor-chat outreach, TikTok through native comments under top crypto creators, CoinMarketCap through daily community comments, and /biz/ through manual native threads by a team active there since 2020.

Will it look organic?

That is the whole point. The tactics are built to feel native to each surface, timed and dispersed to mirror genuine community response instead of an obvious blast. Threads on /biz/ are written manually, TikTok comments feel native, and outreach seeds real conversations rather than spamming a link.

Is guerrilla enough on its own?

It creates presence and momentum, but it lands best on top of a clean community and real on-chain visibility. We usually run it as part of the wider engine so the attention it generates has somewhere solid to convert.
