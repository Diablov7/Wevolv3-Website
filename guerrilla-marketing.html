Questions we hear a lot

Bigger projects are drowning us out. How do we compete without their budget?

You do not win this by spending more. You win it by being everywhere the conversation happens and by giving people a reason to repeat your name. We make you present and talked about across the surfaces crypto lives on, which is a different game than the one whales are paying to play.

Isn't this the same as hiring a big KOL?

No. A KOL is one loud voice for a moment. This is a steady sense that your project is alive everywhere someone looks, coming from many places at once. They work well together, but they solve different problems. One borrows authority, this one builds presence.

We keep getting forgotten. How does this make us memorable?

Being forgettable usually means people saw you once and never again. We fix the never again. When your name keeps turning up in the places people already spend their time, you stop being a project they scrolled past and start being one they recognize.

Will this look like obvious spam?

That would defeat the point. Anything that reads as a blast makes you look desperate, which is worse than being quiet. Everything is built to feel like it belongs where it lands, timed and spread out so it reads as real people talking, not a campaign shouting.

Is this enough on its own?

It creates the noise and the momentum, but noise needs somewhere to land. It works best when the people it sends your way arrive at a real community and a project that checks out. We usually run it alongside the rest so the attention has somewhere solid to go.
