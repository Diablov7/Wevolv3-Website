Ads placement FAQ

What counts as an ad placement in crypto?

Not banner ads. Placement on the surfaces investors actually check before they buy: CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko watchlists and trending, Dexscreener and Dextools chart signals, X trending, and premium spots like CMC Most Visited and Phantom Wallet trending. Pure visibility where the decision is made.

Why does placement beat shouting louder on Twitter?

Retail investors rarely buy on day one. They research for days, and they research on the aggregators. Being present on CMC, CoinGecko, Dexscreener and inside the wallet keeps you in their field of vision through the whole research window, which is where consideration turns into conviction.

Do launch-day reactions and watchlists really matter?

First impressions on Dexscreener are permanent. Strong reactions on day one signal conviction before anyone reads the chart, and low ones act as a silent red flag. Watchlists are self-reinforcing too: cross the threshold and you surface in CMC's Trending Watchlists tab, which compounds discovery with no extra spend.

What is the highest-intent placement you offer?

CMC Most Visited and Phantom Wallet trending. CoinMarketCap's home page draws over 12M unique monthly visitors, and Phantom's trending shows the moment a user opens the Swap tab. That is top-of-funnel real estate in front of people with capital ready to deploy, at the exact moment of decision.

Can you keep our aggregator profiles accurate?

Yes. We refresh and correct Dexscreener and CoinGecko profiles so your data shows accurately across market screens. A stale or incomplete profile signals negligence and loses buyers who are already on your page, so this is quiet work that quietly protects conversions.
