Questions we hear a lot

We spent a fortune on banner ads and got nothing. Why?

Because most crypto buyers never see those banners, and the ones who do are not deciding there. They decide on the aggregator, the chart, inside the wallet, in the minutes before they buy. If you are not visible in that window, the spend buys impressions and no users.

How do you reach a crypto audience I cannot target on normal ad networks?

We do not try to. We place you on the surfaces this audience already lives on and trusts, so you show up where they research instead of chasing them across networks that were never built for crypto.

How will I know the money actually turned into users?

That is the whole point. You end up with a line between what you spent and the people who showed up, instead of a report full of impressions you cannot connect to a single real buyer.

Does being visible where buyers research really change anything?

Yes. Buyers rarely act the first time they hear about you. They circle back and check the same places for days. Being present through that whole window is what turns a passing look into a decision.

Our profile data is a mess. Does that cost us buyers?

It does, quietly. Someone lands on your page ready to look closer, sees stale or wrong data, and walks. We keep what buyers see accurate so the interest you paid to create does not leak out at the last step.
