Listings FAQ

Which listings do you handle?

The ones that move discovery and trust: CoinMarketCap and CoinGecko, major trackers like Coinsniper, Coinscope and GemFinder, Chinese and Asian market listings, StockTwits for traditional-market reach, and exchange listings. From the aggregators to the exchange, in one place.

Why is a CMC or CoinGecko listing worth it?

They are the first places serious investors and launchpad partners check. A missing or incomplete listing reads as a ghost project and quietly disqualifies you from real capital. Being listed, with accurate data, is table stakes for being taken seriously.

What is supply verification and do I need it?

CoinMarketCap verifies your circulating supply against their standard so your market cap displays correctly. It matters because an unverified or wrong supply distorts your numbers and undermines trust. We handle the standard process, and a fast-track option exists when a deadline is tight.

Can you reach Asian and traditional markets?

Yes. We cover Chinese listing platforms like Moontok, cntoken and hkdefi with translation and local compliance included, and StockTwits to reach traditional stock-market investors alongside the crypto audience.

What about exchange listings?

We handle negotiations, documentation and the technical integration for reputable exchanges. Scope varies by exchange, so this one is planned on request around your token, your timeline and your goals.
