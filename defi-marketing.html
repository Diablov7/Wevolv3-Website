DeFi marketing FAQ

How do you grow TVL without attracting only mercenary capital?

Incentives bring liquidity in; narrative, trust and utility keep it. We pair points and reward campaigns with education, security proof and community so depositors have reasons to stay after the emissions taper, not just farm and leave.

How important is the security and trust narrative in DeFi?

It's the deciding factor. Users move real money into contracts, so audits, track record and transparent communication do more for conversion than any hype campaign. We make your security story legible and front-and-centre.

Can you run points and incentive campaigns?

Yes. We design points, quests and incentive mechanics that reward the behaviours you actually want, real deposits, long holds, governance participation, and filter out pure farmers, with tracking so you see the ROI.

Do you work with new protocols and established ones?

Both. New protocols get a launch and trust-building runway; established ones get always-on growth, retention and narrative work to defend and grow TVL against competitors.

How do engagements work?

You turn on exactly the levers you need: community, KOL, PR, incentives and content, and scale each up or down as the protocol grows, without a full-service retainer.
