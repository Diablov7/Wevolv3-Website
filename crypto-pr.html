Crypto PR FAQ

What outlets can you get coverage in?

Both tier-1 business and tech press and the crypto-native outlets your audience actually reads. We match the outlet to the goal: credibility for investors, reach for community, rather than chasing logos for their own sake.

Does PR actually move the needle for a crypto project?

Yes, where it counts: trust. Coverage and thought leadership give investors, exchanges and users the third-party credibility they need before committing. PR is the layer that makes your raise, listing or launch believable.

Can you handle FUD or a crisis?

Yes. We help you respond fast and credibly, clear messaging, the right channels, and a plan that protects the community's trust instead of feeding the fire.

How do you time PR around a raise or listing?

We build an announcement calendar so coverage lands when it creates the most leverage, before a raise for credibility, around a listing for demand, at milestones to sustain momentum.

Is it retainer or project-based?

Either. Tailored by design: run a focused announcement push, or keep an always-on PR and thought-leadership engine, and scale it as needed.

Tell us what you're announcing and when. We'll build the PR plan around it.
